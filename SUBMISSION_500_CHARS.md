Physical AI Crash-Test Lab is a scenario-driven validation platform on NVIDIA Omniverse/Isaac Sim. It generates controlled scene variations with ground-truth labels, finds where a perception model fails (low light, occlusion, camera angle), creates targeted synthetic training data, and proves improvement on a fixed suite via a versioned safety-coverage report. Demo: warehouse helmet detection. GTM: Presidio simulation environments, synthetic-data engineering, managed validation.
